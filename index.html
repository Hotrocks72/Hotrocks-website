About Hot Rocks

Hot Rocks – A Salute to The Rolling Stones is a Houston, Texas-based Rolling Stones tribute band bringing the legendary energy, sound and swagger of The Rolling Stones to the stage.

From iconic hits to deep-cut fan favorites, Hot Rocks delivers an authentic, high-energy Rolling Stones concert experience celebrating the music of one of the greatest rock and roll bands of all time.

Hot Rocks performs throughout Texas at festivals, theaters, live music venues, corporate events, private events, breweries, wineries and special events.
